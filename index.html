Effective Date: February 20, 2026

E&C Courier cares about your privacy. We follow Canadian privacy laws (PIPEDA) to keep your information safe while you use our app and website.

2. How We Share Data

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone. We only share data with Google Firebase to run the technical side of our service, or if the law requires us to.

You have the "right to be forgotten." If you want us to delete your personal information, just email us at admin@eandccourier.ca. We will clear your data from our systems within 30 days.
