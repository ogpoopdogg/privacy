Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 20, 2026

E&C Courier ("the Company," "we," "us") is committed to the protection of user data and adherence to the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). This policy governs data collection within the E&C Courier application and associated Firebase services.

1. Scope of Data Acquisition

  • Identity Data: Legal name, verified email address, and telephonic contact information required for service authentication.
  • Geolocation Telemetry: Precise location tracking is restricted to **authorized Drivers** during active duty cycles. Customer location is processed only at the point of delivery specification and is not tracked persistently.
  • Infrastructure Logs: Technical metadata (device identifiers, IP addresses, crash logs) is processed via **Google Firebase** to ensure platform integrity and uptime.

2. Data Processing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or lease user databases to third parties. Data is shared with Google Firebase solely for the technical execution of service. We reserve the right to disclose information if required by Canadian law or to protect the vital interests of our users.

3. Data Retention & Erasure (The "Right to be Forgotten")

Users may invoke their right to data deletion at any time. Upon receipt of a formal request to admin@eandccourier.ca, the Company will purge all PII (Personally Identifiable Information) from active production databases within thirty (30) days, subject to statutory record-keeping requirements.
